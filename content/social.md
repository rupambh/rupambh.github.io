## My World and Hobbies

Apart from spending time with my work and my family, I love watching all kinds of sports (with a slight predilection towards football - not the American kind!), listening to a broad range of Indian and world music, reading basically any literature that I can get my hands on, and last but not least, *quizzing*. I was a founding member of the quizzing fraternity *Sheyal Pundits* in ISI Kolkata, and was a graduate member of the UMich Quizbowl team.

Here are a few other things I am passionate about:
- **Theatre & Sound**: Between May 2022 and October 2024, I was part of the **Michigan Literary and Theatrical Society (MILITS)** in the capacity of a sound projectionist. I was involved in four productions—*Sararattir*, *Opriyo Sotyo*, *Ghughudanga*, and *Durghatona*—all of which were performed at several Bengali/Indian theatre festivals around the US.
- **Music & Audio Dramas**: My wife Dishari and I run a YouTube channel where we present renditions of our favorite songs. We are also members of a group called **Kochi Songsod** that produces Bengali audio dramas and other online content.
- **Writing**: My popular science writing in Bengali includes a career guidance piece published in the collection *Bigyaner Jogot* during the 2023 Kolkata International Book Fair.

Feel free to connect with me on my [Facebook profile](https://www.facebook.com/rupam.bhattacharyya1995/) (I do have Twitter and Instagram, but am really inactive there!) to chat about books, sports, or music!
